How can we help?

Find answers to common questions and learn how to set up Lectra so you can plan lectures, exams and meetings with ease.

Getting started

1

Create your account

Go to Sign up and enter your full name, email address and a password of at least 8 characters. Add your department and institution so your profile is ready for colleagues to recognise.

2

Set your preferences

Open Profile & settings to set your timezone, default reminder and dark mode. Turn on push notifications so reminders reach you even when the app is closed.

3

Create your first schedule

Click New Schedule (or the blue “+” button on mobile). Add a title, category, date and time, then pick reminders — for example 30 minutes and 1 day before.

4

Install Lectra as an app

Lectra is a PWA. On your phone, open the browser menu and choose Add to Home Screen; on desktop Chrome click the install icon in the address bar. Lectra then launches like a native app and can work offline.

Frequently asked questions

Schedules

How do I create a schedule?

Click New Schedule in the sidebar (or the blue “+” button on mobile). Fill in a title, choose a category, set the date and start time, then save. You can add a description, location, end time, repeat rule, priority and status at any time.

Can I edit or delete a schedule after creating it?

Yes. Click a schedule on the Calendar to see its details, then choose Edit to change it or Delete to remove it. You can also open a schedule from the Dashboard or History and edit it from there.

What do the repeat options do?

Choose Daily, Weekly, Monthly or Yearly to repeat the schedule automatically, or Custom for a schedule that is set on a different rule. Select Does not repeat for a one-off event.

What do the statuses mean?

Upcoming events have not happened yet, Completed events have, Cancelled ones are no longer taking place and Rescheduled marks an event that moved to a new date or time. Use the History page to filter by status.

Calendar

How does the calendar work?

The Calendar shows a full month. Days that have schedules are highlighted — click a day to see its full details in the panel on the right. Use the arrows to move between months and Today to jump back to the current date. On mobile you can also swipe left or right to change months.

Why is a date highlighted but shows no title?

To keep the month view clean, Lectra highlights dates that have schedules instead of printing every title on the grid. Click the highlighted date to open its details, including times, location and actions to edit or delete.

How do I add a schedule from the calendar?

Click any day that has no schedules and press Add schedule in the details panel — the date is pre-filled for you. Or use New Schedule at any time and pick the date yourself.

Reminders & notifications

How do reminders work?

While creating or editing a schedule, tick the times you want to be reminded (for example “10 min”, “30 min” or “1 day” before). You can select more than one. If you pick none, your default reminder from Profile & settings is used automatically.

How do I get push notifications?

Open Profile & settings, enable Push notifications and approve the browser permission prompt. Reminders are then delivered to your device even when the app is closed. Push needs an HTTPS connection and supported browser.

Where do I see notifications?

Click the bell icon in the top bar. Unread notifications show a numbered badge. You can mark a single notification as read, mark all as read, or open the Notifications page from the sidebar to manage them.

Why am I not receiving push notifications?

Check that push is enabled in Profile & settings, that you approved the browser permission prompt, and that your browser and internet connection allow notifications. On iOS, notifications also need the app to be added to your Home Screen. If it still fails, contact support with the details shown next to the push toggle.

Profile, app & install

How do I install Lectra as an app?

On Android / Chrome: open the browser menu and choose Add to Home Screen. On iPhone / iPad: tap the Share button and choose Add to Home Screen. On desktop Chrome: click the install icon in the address bar. Lectra then runs full-screen and can work offline.

How do I change my timezone or default reminder?

Go to Profile & settings. The timezone controls how times appear across the app, and the default reminder is applied to any new schedule where you do not choose reminders manually. Press Save settings when you are done.

How do I switch between light and dark mode?

Click the sun / moon icon in the top bar to toggle dark mode instantly, or set it permanently under Profile & settings > Dark mode.

How do I find a past schedule?

Open the History page, type a keyword (title, location or category) into the search box and optionally filter by status or category. Clear the filters to see everything.

I forgot my password or cannot sign in.

If you cannot sign in, double-check your email and password spelling, and make sure you confirmed your password correctly when signing up. Password recovery is not yet available in the app so please contact support and an administrator will reset your account for you.

Is my data secure?

Passwords are stored securely hashed and every state-changing action is protected with a session-bound security token. Your schedules are only visible to you while signed in.
