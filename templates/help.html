Getting started

1

Create your account

Go to Sign up and enter your full name, email address and a password of at least 8 characters. Add your department and institution so your profile is ready for colleagues to recognise.

2

Open the defence roster

Go to Project Defence. The admin’s posted roster lists every Lv400 presentation across both venues, grouped by day and ordered by time.

3

Tick the defences you’ll attend

For each defence you are interested in, switch on Notify me and pick a reminder lead time (e.g. 30 minutes). Your picks show on the Dashboard under My upcoming picks.

4

Install Lectra as an app

Lectra is a PWA. On your phone, open the browser menu and choose Add to Home Screen; on desktop Chrome click the install icon in the address bar. Lectra then launches like a native app.

Frequently asked questions

Defences

What is the defence roster?

It is the full timetable of Lv400 project defences posted by an administrator. Every presentation lists the student, project title, date, time and venue (defences take place across two venues).

How do I mark that I want to attend a defence?

On Project Defence, switch on Notify me next to a presentation and choose how early you want to be reminded. Switch it off again to remove your pick.

Where do I see the defences I picked?

Use the My picks filter on the Project Defence page, or check My upcoming picks on the Dashboard.

What do the statuses mean?

A defence is Scheduled until its time passes, then it is marked Done. Cancelled defences are no longer taking place and produce no reminders.

Reminders & notifications

When will I be reminded?

You pick the lead time when you tick a defence from “At start” up to two hours before. The notification names the student and project (and the venue, when the roster includes one) so you know which defence is next.

How do I get push notifications?

Open Profile & settings, enable Push notifications and approve the browser permission prompt. Reminders are then delivered to your device even when the app is closed. Push needs an HTTPS connection and supported browser.

Where do I see notifications?

Click the bell icon in the top bar. Unread notifications show a numbered badge. You can mark a single notification as read, mark all as read, or open the Notifications page from the sidebar to manage them.

Why am I not receiving push notifications?

Check that push is enabled in Profile & settings, that you approved the browser permission prompt, and that your browser and internet connection allow notifications. On iOS, notifications also need the app to be added to your Home Screen. If it still fails, contact support with the details shown next to the push toggle.

For admins

Who is the admin?

The very first account created on the app becomes admin, and any email listed in the server’s ADMIN_EMAILS setting is also promoted on sign-in.

How do I upload the roster?

Open Roster, type the venue the file covers (e.g. Hall A) — it is applied to every row — then upload a CSV, TSV, XLSX or Word (.docx) file. Column names are recognised automatically, so use whatever your spreadsheet or document uses — for example Name, Topics, Session/Date, and Time Schedule (a range like 10:00 AM - 10:45 AM in one cell is fine). You review the parsed rows and confirm before anything is saved. Rows the app can’t read (for example a date or time it doesn’t recognise) are skipped and listed so you can fix and re-upload.

Can I fix a single defence?

Yes. On the Roster page each row has Edit to change details, Cancel/Reinstate to toggle its status, and Delete to remove it. You can also add a single defence manually with Add a defence.

Can I remove the whole roster at once?

Yes. The Danger zone at the bottom of the Roster page has a Clear the entire roster button. Use it to undo a roster you didn’t mean to upload — it deletes every defence and everyone’s picks, and cancels their pending reminders, so only do this when you’re sure.

Profile, app & install

How do I install Lectra as an app?

On Android / Chrome: open the browser menu and choose Add to Home Screen. On iPhone / iPad: tap the Share button and choose Add to Home Screen. On desktop Chrome: click the install icon in the address bar. Lectra then runs full-screen and can work offline.

How do I switch between light and dark mode?

Click the sun / moon icon in the top bar to toggle dark mode instantly, or set it permanently under Profile & settings > Dark mode.

I forgot my password or cannot sign in.

If you cannot sign in, double-check your email and password spelling, and make sure you confirmed your password correctly when signing up. Password recovery is not yet available in the app so please contact support and an administrator will reset your account for you.

Is my data secure?

Passwords are stored securely hashed and every state-changing action is protected with a session-bound security token. Only administrators can change the roster; your picks are personal to your account.

Still need help?

If you cannot find an answer here, contact the Lectra support team and we will get back to you as soon as possible. Please include a short description of the issue and the steps you took.
